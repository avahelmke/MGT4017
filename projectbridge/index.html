What is ProjectBridge? ＋

ProjectBridge is a platform connecting university students with businesses for short, paid projects. Students gain practical experience while businesses access emerging talent for specific needs.

Who can create a student account? ＋

University students interested in building skills, gaining experience and working on short-term projects can create a student profile.

Are projects paid? ＋

ProjectBridge is designed around paid opportunities. Each listing should clearly explain the compensation, scope and expected timeline before a student applies.

How do businesses post a project? ＋

Create a business account, add your project details, required skills, timeline and payment information, then submit the listing for review.

How does ProjectBridge build trust? ＋

The planned platform includes account verification, clear project information, communication tools, support and reviews after project completion.
