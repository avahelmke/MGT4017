# ProjectBridge — Business model and prototype scope

## Start-up concept

ProjectBridge connects university students with businesses for short, paid projects. Students gain real-world experience, skills and portfolio pieces. Businesses gain affordable and flexible access to emerging talent for specific business needs.

## Business Model Canvas summary

| Building block | ProjectBridge |
|---|---|
| Customer segments | University students seeking experience; startups, SMEs and other businesses needing short-term project support. |
| Value propositions | Paid, flexible experience for students; affordable access to emerging talent for businesses. |
| Channels | Website, university career services, student societies, LinkedIn and business partnerships. |
| Customer relationships | Self-service accounts, project updates, messaging, support and reviews after project completion. |
| Revenue streams | A fee or commission on completed projects, optional business subscriptions and future university partnerships. |
| Key resources | Platform, software, student and business networks, project listings, matching system and support team. |
| Key activities | Matching students to projects, verifying users, managing payments, supporting projects and maintaining the platform. |
| Key partnerships | Universities, careers departments, startups, SMEs, payment providers and legal/compliance advisers. |
| Cost structure | Website development and hosting, payment processing, marketing, customer support, verification, security and legal compliance. |

## Planned modules

- Homepage: explain the platform and direct visitors to the student or business journey.
- Browse projects: searchable listings with filters.
- Project details: scope, pay, deadline, required skills and application route.
- User dashboard: applications, projects and business listings.
- Accounts and profiles: user information, skills and credibility.
- Payment and reviews: planned payment and project-completion feedback.
- FAQ and support: answers, contact and feedback.

## Scope note

The delivered code is a front-end prototype of the public-facing experience. The internal admin system, secure account management, real payment processing, messaging, dispute handling, verification and analytics are documented as future backend requirements rather than represented as live functionality.
